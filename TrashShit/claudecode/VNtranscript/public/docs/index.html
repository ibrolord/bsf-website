Getting Started

First time here? Start with the basics.

What is the Big Sister Foundation platform? ▼

The Big Sister Foundation (BSF) platform is a website where you can see exactly how the foundation works — where money goes, which children are being supported, what ideas the community has, and how you can get involved. Everything is transparent and open.

The platform has 6 main sections:

💡 You don't need an account to browse. Anyone can view the ledger, scholars, ideas, and forums. You only need to sign in if you want to submit ideas (as a volunteer) or manage data (as an admin).
How to navigate the website ▼

The navigation bar at the top of every page lets you move between sections. It's always visible, even when you scroll down.

bigsisterfoundation.org
Big Sister Foundation
Home Ledger Ideas Scholars Forums Volunteer
Click any link to go to that section

  1. Find the navigation bar

    It's the dark green bar at the very top of the page. It stays there even when you scroll.

  2. Click on any page name

    Click "Ledger" to see finances, "Ideas" to see community proposals, "Scholars" to see supported children, "Forums" to join discussions, or "Volunteer" to apply.

  3. Click "Big Sister Foundation" to go home

    The logo on the left always takes you back to the main homepage.

📱 On mobile: The navigation works the same way. The links may appear smaller but they're all still there at the top of the screen.

See every naira that comes in and goes out. Full transparency.

How to read the financial ledger ▼

The ledger shows all money coming into and going out of the foundation. Anyone can view it — no sign-in needed.

bigsisterfoundation.org/ledger/

  1. Go to the Ledger page

    Click "Ledger" in the navigation bar.

  2. Understand the layout

    Entries are grouped by month. Each entry shows the date, category (e.g. Grant, Food, Education), the amount, and a description. Green amounts are money coming in. Orange/red amounts are money going out.

  3. Filter by type

    Use the tabs at the top: "All Transactions" shows everything, "Money In" shows only income (grants, donations), "Money Out" shows only expenses.

  4. View the flow diagram

    The Sankey diagram at the top of the page gives you a visual picture of where money comes from and where it goes — follow the flowing lines from sources on the left to programs on the right.

🔒 Radical transparency: The ledger is public by design. BSF believes you should be able to see exactly how every naira is used.
How to add a ledger entry (volunteers) ▼

Signed-in volunteers can record financial transactions to keep the ledger up to date.

  1. Sign in on the Ledger page

    Click "Volunteer Sign In" and enter your email and password.

  2. Click "+ Add Entry"

    A form pops up asking for the transaction details.

  3. Choose "Money In" or "Money Out"

    Click the toggle at the top of the form. This changes the available categories.

  4. Fill in the details

    Amount: The amount in naira.
    Category: For income: Grant, Partnership, Event Proceeds, Donation. For expenses: Food, Education, Healthcare, Counselling, Supplies, Operations.
    Description: Brief explanation of the transaction.
    Date: When it happened.
    Program: Which program it relates to (optional).

  5. Click "Add to Ledger"

    The entry appears immediately in the ledger, visible to everyone.

    Click "Scholars" in the navigation bar.

  2. Browse the table

    Each row shows a scholar's name, school, class, pathway, performance rating, extracurriculars, status, and total spending. On mobile, this appears as cards instead of a table.

  3. Use the filters

How to add a new scholar (volunteers) ▼

  1. Sign in on the Scholars page

    Click "Volunteer Sign In" and enter your credentials.

  2. Click "+ Add Scholar"

    A form appears with all the fields for a new scholar.

  3. Fill in the details

    First Name & Last Initial: For privacy, we use first name + last initial only (e.g., "Amina K.").
    School: Select from the dropdown, or choose "Other" and type a new school name.
    Class/Grade: e.g. "JSS 2" or "Primary 5".
    Enrolled Date: When the child joined BSF.
    Pathway: Which program pathway they belong to.
    Performance: Current academic/personal performance level.
    Extracurriculars: Type an activity and press Enter to add it as a tag. Add as many as needed.
    Status: Active, Graduated, On Hold, or Withdrawn.

  4. Click "Add Scholar"

    The scholar appears in the table immediately.

⚠️ Privacy reminder: Never enter a child's full last name. Always use first name + last initial only. Never upload photos without explicit consent from the family.

  1. Go to the Forums page

    Click "Forums" in the navigation bar.

  2. Click "+ New Thread"

    The green button near the top of the page. A form will pop up.

  3. Enter your name

    Type whatever name you'd like to be known by. Email is optional — it's only used if someone replies and you want to be notified. It's never shown publicly.

💭 Your name is remembered. After your first post, the forum saves your name so you don't have to type it again next time.
How to reply to a thread ▼

  1. Click on any thread

    From the forums list, click on a thread title or card to open it.

  2. Read the discussion

    The original post appears at the top, followed by all replies in order.

  3. Scroll to the reply form

    At the bottom of the page, you'll see "Leave a Reply" with name, email, and message fields.

  4. Enter your name and message

    Type your name (it may already be filled in if you've posted before), write your reply, and click "Post Reply".

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is the platform free to use? ▼
Yes, completely free. There are no fees, subscriptions, or hidden costs. The platform is built and maintained by volunteers.
Can I volunteer from outside Lagos? ▼
Yes! While in-person volunteering happens in Lagos, you can contribute remotely — tutoring online, helping with the platform, fundraising, social media, or mentoring. Apply through the Volunteer page and mention your location.
Why can't I see a child's full name? ▼
To protect the children's privacy, we only display first names and last initials (e.g., "Amina K."). Full names are kept confidential and only accessible to authorized BSF staff.
How do I report something inappropriate on the forums? ▼
Contact the BSF team directly via WhatsApp or email with details of the post or thread. The team can remove inappropriate content from the admin dashboard.
How can I nominate a child for BSF support? ▼
External nominations are welcome. Contact the BSF team via WhatsApp with the family's details. The team visits every family before enrollment to understand their situation and needs.
Is the financial data real? ▼
Yes. The ledger reflects actual transactions. BSF is committed to radical financial transparency — every naira that comes in or goes out is recorded and publicly visible. This is core to how the foundation operates.
I'm having trouble with the website. Who do I contact? ▼
Reach out on WhatsApp or email princebolajibreeze@gmail.com. Include a description of the problem and what page you were on.
Can I donate to the foundation? ▼
Contact the BSF team via WhatsApp to discuss how you can contribute. All donations are recorded transparently in the public ledger.
